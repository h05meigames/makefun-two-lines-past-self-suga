Two lines to a past self for the [u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/0ene8b3q) — line one warns about one specific moment, line two says why it mattered and what changed because of it.

## The entry

> Don't walk out of the Bekasi warehouse at 11 p.m. on your first night, however loud the foreman shouts.
> The supervisor who watches you finish that shift puts your name forward for the forklift course a month later, and that licence is what gets you out of lifting boxes for good.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the two lines to
`/output/entry.txt` — exactly two lines, UTF-8, 1 to 280 characters, per the kit gate. It
also prints the same text to stdout.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
